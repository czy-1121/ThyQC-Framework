# Data Use

The released anonymized subset is intended for reproducible thyroid ultrasound quality-control research.

Allowed use:

- Recompute the reported five-task quality-control metrics.
- Compare quality-control models under the same closed vocabulary.
- Inspect acquisition quality labels and failure modes.

Not allowed:

- Patient identification or re-identification.
- Diagnosis, TI-RADS prediction, treatment recommendation, or clinical decision-making.
- Inferring original acquisition dates, hospital workflow, or patient-level metadata.

The labels are visual acquisition quality-control labels, not diagnostic labels.
